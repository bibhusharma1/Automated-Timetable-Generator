Upload CSV Data

Four CSV files define your entire institution — subjects, teachers, rooms, and classes.

📚 subjects.csv
subject_id, subject_name, year, semester, credits, hours_per_week, subject_type, requires_lab, lab_hours_per_week
credits drives morning slot priority. requires_lab=true → placed in lab rooms as 2-hr blocks.
👩‍🏫 teachers.csv
teacher_id, teacher_name, designation, subjects, max_hours_per_day, available_days
subjects = comma-separated IDs like "S501,S502". available_days = "Mon,Tue,Wed,Thu,Fri".
🏫 rooms.csv
room_id, room_name, capacity, room_type, building
room_type = classroom | lab | activity. Labs auto-matched to lab subjects.
🎓 classes.csv
class_id, class_name, year, semester, strength, subjects
semester is key — each semester group is scheduled independently for max score.
